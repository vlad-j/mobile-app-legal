Delete your Travel Buddy account

How to delete your Travel Buddy account and its data, from the app or without it, and what deleting removes.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Contents

  1. In the app
  2. Without the app
  3. What is deleted
  4. Trips you share
  5. What is kept elsewhere

1In the app

Open Profile, tap Account details, then Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm. The deletion runs straight away, and this device is signed out when it finishes.

If the app cannot finish the deletion on your device, it opens a message to our support address instead, and a person completes it for you.

2Without the app

Write to us from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account":

little.giant.apps@gmail.com

A person reads and processes every request within 5 business days. If the message comes from a different address, we will ask you to confirm from the one on the account before deleting anything.

3What is deleted

Your account and the way you sign in to it; your profile, including your name, photo, home country, emergency contact and loyalty numbers; every trip you are the only member of, with everything in it; your journal entries and their photos; the travel documents in your wallet; and the bookings you imported.

Deletion cannot be undone.

4Trips you share

If you own a trip that other people are still on, it passes to the next member instead of being deleted, so their plans are not lost with your account. Things you added to a trip that other members are still on stay with that trip for them, no longer linked to your account.

5What is kept elsewhere

The copy of your trips that Travel Buddy keeps on your phone for offline use stays there until you uninstall the app. Booking imports were read by Anthropic's Claude API; how long Anthropic keeps what it was sent is set by Anthropic's own data-retention policy, as our Privacy Policy explains.
