One account is meant for one person. To travel with others, share the trip from inside the app — that is what trip members are for — rather than sharing a login.

3What Travel Buddy is, and is not

Travel Buddy is a travel planner and journal. It holds the trips you plan, the bookings you record, the money you spend and the notes you write, and it reminds you about things you have already told it.

It is not a booking service. It does not buy, book, change or cancel flights, stays or anything else, it takes no payment for travel, and it is not a travel agent. Every booking you record here was made somewhere else, and only the provider you made it with can change it.

It is not a live source of truth for your travel. Times, routes, check-in windows and status come from what you or your import recorded — not from an airline, railway or hotel feed. Check the provider's own app or website before you travel. Weather and exchange rates come from the third parties named in the Privacy Policy and are indicative only.
